🚀 What is Doblarr?

Doblarr is a powerful self-hosted tool that automatically translates and dubs your favorite movies and TV shows. It works seamlessly alongside Radarr and Sonarr to provide translated audio tracks with advanced voice cloning technology. Whether you watch through Plex, Jellyfin, or any other media library, Doblarr makes foreign content feel like it was originally recorded in your language.

Automatic translation and dubbing of your entire media library using artificial intelligence.
